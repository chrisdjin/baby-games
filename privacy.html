Privacy Policy 🐣

BabyPro · Last updated 5 September 2026

We do not collect any data.

BabyPro is a collection of simple learning games made for toddlers and young children. Protecting children's privacy is our highest priority. This app is designed to collect no personal information whatsoever.

What we collect

Nothing. BabyPro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from you or your child. Specifically:

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contact details.
  • No photos, microphone, camera, or location data.
  • No account or sign-in of any kind is required or offered.
  • No usage analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising identifiers.

How the app works

  • BabyPro runs entirely on your device and works fully offline.
  • It makes no network connections and sends no data to us or anyone else.
  • Spoken words are produced by your device's built-in speech feature; nothing you or your child says is recorded or transmitted.
  • Any settings you choose (such as the age band or play-time limit) are stored only on your own device and never leave it.

Advertising and third parties

BabyPro contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no third-party software development kits, analytics, or services. There are no links that take a child out of the app.

Children's privacy

Because BabyPro collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (or any age). The app is intended to be used under the supervision of a parent or guardian, and includes a parental gate (a math question) protecting its settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
